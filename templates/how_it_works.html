Process

How AutoValue AI estimates a price

The application applies a fitted preprocessing pipeline and a saved regression model. It does not retrain on every prediction.

01

Enter available car specifications

Dropdowns are filled from values observed in the training dataset. Brand, model, and variant stay linked so unsupported combinations are harder to submit.

02

The application preprocesses the inputs

Numeric fields are scaled with the training scaler. Categorical fields are one-hot encoded with the categories learned during training. Unknown categories are ignored rather than crashing the request.

03

The trained model estimates the price

Linear Regression, Random Forest, and histogram-based Gradient Boosting were compared on a held-out test set. The selected model is stored with Joblib and loaded at inference time.

04

The result is displayed

The estimated selling price is formatted in Indian rupees. An optional range uses test-set mean absolute error as a practical spread, not a confidence interval.
