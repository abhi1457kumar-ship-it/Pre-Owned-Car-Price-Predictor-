About

Data-driven estimates with explicit limits

Purpose

AutoValue AI helps people form a first view of a used car's likely selling price using historical listings from the attached Indian used-car dataset. It is a decision-support tool, not a certified valuation service.

Data-driven approach

The application trains regression models on the CSV you provided. Feature fields in the form match columns that actually exist: brand, model, variant, manufacturing year, kilometres driven, fuel type, transmission, previous owners, engine capacity, mileage, condition, and city. The target is selling price in Indian rupees.

Limitations

  • Listings do not capture accident history, service records, insurance, or negotiation outcomes.
  • Luxury and mass-market cars sit in the same model, so residuals can be large on uncommon vehicles.
  • A 2025 manufacturing year appears in the source file; treat future-dated rows as part of the given data, not as a live market feed.
  • The estimate is not an official appraisal and should not be the only input to a purchase or sale.
